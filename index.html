    Questions families ask

    Before you call us

    How early should we book?

    Twelve months for a winter wedding in a popular hall. Six months is workable for smaller events or quieter seasons.

    Do you plan the ladies' and men's sections separately?

    Yes. Each hall has its own coordinator and vendor team, working from one shared timeline so the zaffa and dinner land at the right moment in both.

    Can we use our own photographer or florist?

    Of course. We add them to the call sheet and manage them like any other vendor.

    Do you work outside the Eastern Province?

    We regularly plan in Riyadh and Bahrain. Travel and crew accommodation are added at cost.
